About Festopia

Festopia is our college's annual cultural and technical festival. It brings together students from different colleges to showcase their talents, share ideas, learn new skills, and enjoy exciting competitions. From coding challenges to dance performances, Festopia offers something for everyone.

Rules and Guidelines

  1. Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  2. Register for events before the registration deadline.
  3. Participants must report at the venue on time.
  4. Follow the instructions given by event coordinators.
  5. Maintain discipline and respect all participants.
  6. Plagiarism and unfair practices are not allowed.
  7. Follow the time limits for each competition.
  8. The judges' decisions will be final.
  9. Keep the campus clean and use dustbins.
  10. Contact the organizing team for assistance.
